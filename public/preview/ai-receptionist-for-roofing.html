Why done for you matters

An AI receptionist is not something you set up once. Prices change. Hours change. A new service gets added. A caller asks something nobody planned for. Someone has to listen to the calls and fix what was missed.

If that someone is you, it happens at 9 pm after a full day on the tools, or it does not happen at all.

That is why we recommend against tools you have to maintain yourself, such as Rosie, Goodcall, My AI Front Desk, Dialzara, Upfirst, Simple Phones and NextPhone. They are good software. But an owner should be doing what they do best, which is running the business, not getting the tech right.

Common questions

How much does an AI receptionist cost for a roofing company?

Tools you look after yourself start around $30 a month. A receptionist that is built and run for you costs about $300 to $500 a month.

What is the best AI receptionist for a small roofing company?

One that is done for you. A small company has nobody spare to look after software, so choose a service where a team sets it up, makes the changes and checks the calls.

What is the difference between a tool and a done-for-you service?

With a tool, you get a login and you keep it right yourself. With a done-for-you service, a team builds it, changes it when your business changes and reviews the calls.

Can an AI receptionist book jobs into my CRM?

Yes. A good one books the visit while the caller is on the line and puts it in your calendar or CRM. Check whether that is included in the price or costs more.

What happens with emergency calls?

You decide what counts as urgent, such as an active leak or storm damage. Those calls are put through to you or your on-call crew.

Is an AI receptionist better than an answering service?

An answering service takes a message and charges by the minute. An AI receptionist books the job, answers several callers at once and costs the same in a busy month.

Will callers know they are talking to AI?

A good one sounds natural and says it is an assistant if asked. Most callers care that their problem is booked in.

Can it answer only after hours?

Yes. Most companies start by sending it the calls they miss and the calls that come in after hours.

What should I ask before I sign up?

Ask what 300 calls a month will cost, whether there is a charge per minute, who makes changes after launch, and whether a person listens to your calls.

How long does it take to set up?

It depends on the service. Ours takes 24 to 48 hours from the first call to your phone being covered.
