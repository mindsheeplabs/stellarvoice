The best AI receptionist for HVAC companies in 2026

By the Stellar Voice Agents team · Updated 10 November 2026 · 5 minute read
The short answer

The best AI receptionist for an HVAC company answers every call day and night, books the visit instead of taking a message, knows an emergency from a tune-up, and is looked after by someone other than you.

We are Stellar Voice Agents and we are one of the options below. Every price here comes from the company's own website.

What good looks like

Six things to check before you choose one.

1. It answers every callNights, weekends, and three callers at once on the first hot day.
2. It books the visitThe job lands in your calendar or CRM. You do not ring anyone back.
3. It knows an emergencyNo heat in winter or a gas smell goes straight to your on-call tech.
4. It sounds like a personCallers stay on the line and give their details.
5. It fits how you workYour services, prices, hours and service area, in your CRM.
6. Someone keeps it sharpCalls are reviewed and it is updated when your business changes.

Your three options

A tool you set up yourself

Rosie, Goodcall, My AI Front Desk, Dialzara and Smith.ai all sell these, from about $30 to $150 a month. Good value if someone in your office likes setting up software and has time to look after it.

A human answering service

Real people take the call and pass on a message. Friendly, but priced by the minute: Abby is $164 for 50 minutes of talk time, so a busy month adds up fast.

An AI receptionist run for you

A team builds it, connects it and keeps improving it. You pay a flat monthly price and never log in. This is what most owner-run companies want.

Which one is right for you

Owner-run, up to about 15 people

  • Choose a run-for-you receptionist.
  • Pick one built for trades, so it knows a no-cool call from a quote.

You have an office manager who likes software

  • A set-up-yourself tool will cost less.
  • Budget a few hours a month to keep it right.

Common questions

How much does an AI receptionist cost for an HVAC company?

Set-up-yourself tools start around $30 a month. A receptionist that is built and run for you costs about $300 to $500 a month.

Will callers know they are talking to AI?

A good one sounds natural and says it is an assistant if asked. Most callers care that their problem is booked in.

How long does it take to set up?

With a run-for-you service, about a week from the first call to your phone being covered.
