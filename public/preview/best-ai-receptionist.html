The short answer

For most HVAC companies, the best AI receptionist is one that a specialist team builds and runs for you. The do-it-yourself tools are cheaper, but someone in your business has to set them up and keep them working.

What looking after one really takes

An AI receptionist is not set once and left. Someone has to write what it says, listen to calls, fix what it gets wrong, and update it when your hours, prices or service area change. For most owners that someone is you, at night.

How to choose

Pick a done-for-you team if

  • You are on the tools most of the day.
  • Nobody in the office wants to manage software.
  • A missed call costs you a job.

Pick a tool if

  • You have someone technical with spare time.
  • You enjoy tuning things yourself.
  • Lowest price matters most.
