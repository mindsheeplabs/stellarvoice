Questions owners ask

Will my customers know it is not a person?

It sounds natural and it says it is an assistant if asked. Most callers just want their problem booked in.

What does it cost?

$297 a month for up to 300 calls. Set-up is $100, or free on a 3 month plan. There is no charge per minute.

What happens if I go over 300 calls?

Nothing changes on your bill. If you are over two months in a row, we talk with you about moving to Growth. You are never charged per call.

Is there a contract?

No. It is month to month. Choose a 3 month plan and set-up is free.

Can I try it before I pay?

Yes. We build your own receptionist for your company and send you a number to call.

What if it is an emergency?

You tell us what counts as urgent. Those calls are put through to you or your on-call tech.

Can a caller speak to a real person?

Yes. You decide when calls are put through to you or your team, and who gets them.

Does it work with my CRM?

Yes. We connect it for you, and it is included in the price. Bookings and call notes land where your team already works.

Can it answer only the calls I miss?

Yes. Most owners start that way. Your phone rings first, and the receptionist picks up when you cannot, and after hours.

How many calls can it take at once?

Two at the same time on Standard and five on Growth.

What if it does not know the answer?

It does not guess. It takes the caller's details and passes the question to you.

Can I hear the calls?

Yes. Every call is recorded and written up, and you get a text and an email after each one.

How do I change something?

Tell us. New hours, new prices, a new service or a special offer. We make the change for you.

How long does set-up take?

24 to 48 hours from our first call to your phone being covered.

Do I keep my phone number?

Yes. Calls you cannot answer are forwarded. Nothing changes for your customers.

What happens if I cancel?

You turn off call forwarding and your phone works as it did before. Your number stays yours.
