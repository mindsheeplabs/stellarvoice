Three ways to tell the story

Same offer, three different openings.

Each one bets on a different reason a tradesman would trust us. They are deliberately far apart, so it is easy to say which one feels right. The current live home page is here for comparison.

Version A

The moment

Opens at 9:14 pm with a customer whose AC has died, and shows the same call ending two ways.

The bet: they feel the lost job before they hear about us.

Tone: dramatic, dark, a story.

What we believe: "You should not have to choose between doing the job and answering the phone."

Open version A Version B

The people

Opens with Gary and David by name, then a letter to the owner about why we do it all for them.

The bet: they buy from people, and nobody else shows theirs.

Tone: personal, plain, a handshake.

What we believe: "You did not start a company to manage software."

Open version B Version C

The proof

Opens with the real call and a play button, then walks through what it did and a real client story.

The bet: they trust what they can hear and check.

Tone: confident, factual, show do not tell.

What we believe: "If we cannot play you the call, we should not ask for your trust."

Questions that will tell us what we want
  • Which opening would make a tradesman stop scrolling?
  • Which of the three "what we believe" lines sounds most like us?
  • Should we show our own faces and names, or stay behind the brand?
  • Is the dark, dramatic look right for us, or too much?
  • Which parts would you lift from one version into another?
  • What is missing from all three?
