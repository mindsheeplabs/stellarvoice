Pricing questions

What does it cost?

$297 a month for up to 300 calls on Standard, or $597 a month for up to 750 calls on Growth. Set-up is $100, or free on a 3 month plan. All prices are in US dollars.

Is there a charge per minute or per call?

No. It is one flat monthly price. A long emergency call costs the same as a short one.

What happens if I go over 300 calls?

Nothing changes on your bill. If you are over two months in a row, we talk with you about moving to Growth. You are never charged per call.

Is there a contract?

No. It is month to month. Choose a 3 month plan and set-up is free.

Is CRM integration extra?

No. Connecting your CRM and calendar is included in the price, and we do it for you.

Can I try it before I pay?

Yes. We build your own receptionist for your company and send you a number to call.

What about outbound calling and following up old leads?

That work is different for every business, so we price it on a call. Book a 15 minute demo and we will give you a figure.
