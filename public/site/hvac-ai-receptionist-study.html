At a glance

The company
An owner-run HVAC company with five trucks and one person in the office. A modelled example.
The problem
About 400 calls a month. The ones that come in after hours, at lunch or on the first hot day go to voicemail.
What we set up
An AI receptionist that answers the calls the office cannot, books the visit and puts emergencies through to the on-call tech.
What the model shows
56 missed calls answered, 14 more jobs booked and about $4,900 in extra work a month, for $297.

The situation

The owner is on a job. The office manager is on the other line, at lunch, or gone home at five. The phone still rings.

CallRail measured a 14% missed-call rate for home services businesses in January 2025. Invoca's 2026 benchmark is harsher: a person answered only 52% of inbound home services calls. We used the lower figure, so this model is the cautious version.

At 400 calls a month, 14% is 56 calls that nobody picks up. Some are suppliers and sales calls. Many are people with a broken system who will ring the next company on the list.

What gets set up, and how long it takes
