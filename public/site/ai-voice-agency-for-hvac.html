Questions HVAC owners ask

What is an AI voice agency?

A company that builds, installs and looks after AI voice agents for you. You do not buy software and set it up yourself. We do the consultation, write and build the agent, connect your CRM, test it, switch it on and keep improving it.

Do you only work with HVAC companies?

We work with HVAC, roofing and plumbing companies across the United States. This page is about what we do for HVAC.

What does it cost for an HVAC company?

$297 a month for up to 300 calls, or $597 a month for up to 750. Set-up is $100, or free on a 3 month plan. There is no charge per minute.

How long does it take to set up?

24 to 48 hours from our first call to your phone being covered.

Will it work with my CRM?

Yes. We connect it for you, and it is included in the price. Bookings and call notes land where your team already works.

What happens on a no-heat or no-cool call at night?

You tell us what counts as urgent. Those calls are put through to you or your on-call tech. Everything else is booked for the morning and you get a text.

Who looks after it once it is live?

We do. A person on our team reviews your real calls every week, every call is checked by AI every day, and when your hours, prices or services change you tell us and it is done.

Is Stellar Voice Agents a US company?

Yes. Stellar Voice Agents LLC is a United States company, registered in Wyoming.
